This database describes its centerlines as GPS points from OpenStreetMap, smoothed by the maintainers. OpenStreetMap contributors are credited at https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright; OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Track widths were extracted from satellite imagery, and its racing lines were generated by minimum-curvature optimization. The maintainers caution that source data quality varies by venue.
